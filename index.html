What is bakers fat?

Bakers fat is an emulsified fat made for yeast-raised doughs such as bread, buns and scones. It lubricates and softens the dough, improves volume and crumb, and keeps bread softer for longer. ELKAY Bakers Fat is made in Nairobi, Kenya.

How much bakers fat should I use per kg of flour?

Use ELKAY Bakers Fat at 2% of flour weight: 2 kg of bakers fat for every 100 kg of flour (1 kg for every 50 kg of flour).

How much does bakers fat cost in Kenya?

ELKAY Bakers Fat costs KSh 2,300 per 10 kg box. Prices are negotiable for bigger volumes, and big discounts are available on larger and regular monthly orders.

Where can I buy bakers fat in Nairobi?

ELKAY Enterprise Limited manufactures bakers fat in Nairobi and supplies more than 60 bakeries across Kenya. Orders are placed by phone; you can choose delivery or collect your order.

What is pan grease used for?

Pan grease is a release agent brushed in a thin coat onto clean bread tins and baking trays before the dough goes in. It stops bread sticking, does not smoke in the oven and keeps tins free of debris, which protects your tins and speeds up production.

How much does pan grease cost?

ELKAY Pan Grease costs KSh 3,800 per 20-litre jerrican, negotiable for bigger volumes.

Are ELKAY products KEBS and Halal certified?

Yes. ELKAY products are KEBS certified and Halal certified, and ELKAY Pan Grease is vegan.

Do you deliver bakery ingredients?

Yes. You can choose delivery to your bakery or collect your order from us. Shipping costs may apply to deliveries and are confirmed when you order.
